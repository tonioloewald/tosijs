2. **(unverified)** The Happy DOM limitation that hid B1 is recorded only in tosijs's `CLAUDE.md` (around line 380). Happy DOM does not route a slotted node's event through the shadow root, so `composedPath()` lacks the slot and ShadowRoot. Every ecosystem repo that unit-tests slotted shadow Components under Happy DOM will write green tests that check nothing.
